Exported SVG files carry a short `<metadata>` note saying they were made with LeatherDraft, and PDFs carry it in their Creator property. It contains no information about you or your device. You may remove it.

### Needs a commercial license

Using the **software itself** for a commercial purpose, with or without changes, for example:

- selling LeatherDraft, a modified version of it, or a product that includes its code;
- offering it, or a modified version, as a hosted or paid service;

- using a modified version of LeatherDraft inside a company.

Modified versions are not licensed for commercial use. A commercial license covers the official LeatherDraft, and may cover changes made by or for the licensee where it says so.

### Getting a commercial license

### Name and logo

"LeatherDraft" and its logo are not covered by the license. A modified version must use a different name and logo, and must not suggest that it is the official LeatherDraft.
